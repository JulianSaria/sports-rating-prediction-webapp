# Collection of hints and advices for the final presentation

### _Nothing is worse than presenting a live demo that suddenly doesn't work anymore_

Solution: Prepare a Video as backup

### _Don't present all single features you have implemented into your prototype._

Less is more! Focus on the most exciting aspects. User registration or login is typically not compelling.

### _Stay on time._

Train your presentation at home and record your time. No audience wants to make extra hours.

### _Captivate the audience_

Pick the audience from the project description and quickly lead them to the project's highlights. Do not expect them to know what topic XY means automatically. Start from the beginning, then promptly go to the core of your project.

### _Write keywords (bullets), not complete sentences, on your slides!_
